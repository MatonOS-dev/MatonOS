# Rules 
## ALL HUMAN CODE AND ASSETS ARE ALLOWED. 
Beyond that, use common sense, If you are writing code by hand then you automatically follow these guidelines. Be respectful, be clear, leave comments and don't be unpleasant. Drama is dumb and I assume we're all adults. 
## DIFFUSION TECH/AI IMAGE AND VIDEO GEN IS BANNED.
Coders have been stealing and reusing code snippets and parts since the dawn of time. The same can't be said about AI image/music/video gen. 
## I CAN'T HOLD A CALCULATOR RESPONSIBLE
Any code you submit, ai genned or not, is your responsibility. You are assumed to at the very least have a general understanding of what your ai agent did, why it did it and the benefits of its process. AI is like a calculator, I am not going to be mad at the calculator when you told it to try dividing by 0.
